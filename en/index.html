STEA — Logistics management and stock tracking

A stock and logistics management application built for STEA Trans Euro Afrika SARL, in Lomé. It covers the whole cycle, from purchases to deliveries, and runs today on the company workstations.

  • Java 17
  • Spring Boot
  • MySQL
  • JWT
  • React
  • Tailwind CSS
  • Recharts
  • Electron
Case study

STEA Trans Euro Afrika runs several warehouses, purchases, deliveries and returns. With no central tool, the information scatters: the real state of the stock becomes uncertain and shortages are found out too late.

What it does

  • Stock you can trust: several warehouses, batches, serial numbers.
  • The whole cycle — orders, deliveries, returns — with no re-keying between documents.
  • The alert before the shortage, not after: thresholds, restocking, notifications.
  • Precise rights: every role sees what concerns it and acts only within its scope.
  • 24 modules in six families, installed in one click on the company workstations.

Afémé — My properties on the map

A web application to record one’s properties — house, land, field, shop — and find them again on an interactive map, sorted by category.

  • Python
  • Flask
  • MySQL
  • Folium
  • Leaflet

A house in the village, a field, a plot in town: people know they own them, far less often exactly where they are. Afémé — “at home” in Ewe — puts them all on a map.

What it does

  • Each property recorded with its name, its category and its location.
  • One click on the map, or “My location”, and the coordinates fill themselves in.
  • One marker colour per category, and filters with counters: the map and the list follow.
  • Personal accounts: everyone sees only their own properties.
